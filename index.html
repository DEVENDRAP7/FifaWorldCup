Privacy Policy

World Cup 2026 - Live Scores · Effective Date: June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the World Cup 2026 - Live Scores mobile application ("the App", "we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the App. By using the App you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information (only if you create an account or sign in)

You can use most of the App as a guest. If you choose to sign in, we collect and process, through Firebase Authentication (a Google service):

  • Email address — for email/password sign-in, or as provided by your Google account when using "Continue with Google".
  • Display name — the name you enter at sign-up, or your Google account name.
  • Profile photo URL — if available from your Google account.
  • A unique user ID — generated by Firebase to identify your account.

1.2 Preferences (stored on your device)

  • Favorite team — the national team you choose to follow.
  • Language preference — if you override your device language.
  • Your voting record — which matches you have voted on, kept locally so you can change or undo a vote.

1.3 Voting Data

When you vote on a match outcome, your choice increments anonymous, aggregate counters stored in our cloud database (Cloud Firestore, a Google service). Votes are stored only as running totals per match (home / draw / away). These totals are not linked to your account, identity, email, or device. We cannot tell who voted for what — only the combined percentages shown in the App.

1.4 Information Collected Automatically

To understand how the App is used and to keep it stable, we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These collect:

  • Usage & interaction data — screens viewed and in-app events (e.g. sign-in, casting a vote), and an app-instance identifier.
  • Crash & diagnostic data — crash reports, stack traces, device model, operating-system version, and app version, used to diagnose and fix problems.
  • Approximate technical data — such as IP address (used by Firebase to derive a coarse, country-level region) and timestamps.

The App uses no advertising SDKs and does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

1.5 Information from Third-Party Data Sources

The App fetches match fixtures, live scores, group standings, and team flags from the public FIFA data API (api.fifa.com). These requests originate from your device and contain no personal identifiers beyond standard HTTP metadata handled by that provider under its own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • Display match schedules, live scores, group standings, and knockout brackets.
  • Create and maintain your account, and keep you signed in across sessions.
  • Send a verification email when you sign up with email/password.
  • Record and aggregate match votes to show community prediction percentages.
  • Personalize content based on your favorite team and language preference.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device (using on-device storage, Hive): favorite team, language preference, your voting record, and session state.
  • In the cloud (Google Firebase): your account credentials and profile (Firebase Authentication), anonymous aggregate vote totals (Cloud Firestore), and usage & crash data (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics).

Firebase data is processed on Google's infrastructure. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy and the Firebase data terms at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared only with Google Firebase, which acts as our service provider to deliver authentication, the voting database, usage analytics, and crash reporting. The App contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

5. Your Choices & Rights

  • Guest mode — use the App without creating an account.
  • Change favorite team / language — anytime from the Profile screen.
  • Clear your votes — remove your local voting record from the Profile screen.
  • Sign out — end your session from the Profile screen.
  • Delete account — permanently delete your account and remove your votes from our database, directly in the App (Profile → Delete Account). This action cannot be undone.
  • Uninstall — removes all data stored locally on your device.

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can exercise deletion directly via "Delete Account", or contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account or it becomes inactive. Local data is retained until you clear it or uninstall the App. Aggregate vote totals are anonymous and may be retained indefinitely as they contain no personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Permissions

The App requests only the Internet permission to fetch live match data and connect to Firebase. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

9. Data Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Account data is protected by Firebase Authentication. The voting database is protected by server-side security rules that allow only vote-count updates and prevent access to any other data. No personal identifier is stored alongside votes.
