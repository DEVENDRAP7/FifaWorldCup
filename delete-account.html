Delete Your Account

FIFA World Cup 2026 · Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how to delete your FIFA World Cup 2026 account and the data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the FIFA World Cup 2026 app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Actions and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm (re-enter your password or re-authenticate with Google).

Your account is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it within 30 days:

  • Email: devendranprajapati07@gmail.com
  • Subject: Delete my account

What is deleted

  • Your account record in Firebase Authentication — email address, display name, profile photo URL, and user ID.
  • Your match votes are removed from our database.
  • All data stored on your device (favorite team, language, local vote record) is cleared when you delete the account in-app, or when you uninstall the app.

What is kept

  • Anonymous aggregate vote totals. Votes are stored only as combined per-match counts and contain no personal information or any link to your identity, so they cannot be tied back to you. These anonymous totals may be retained indefinitely.

Retention

Personal account data is deleted immediately when you use in-app deletion, or within 30 days of an email request. We do not retain personal data beyond this period.
